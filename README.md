# Executioner

<img alt="thumbnail" height="300" src="/assets/thumbnail.png"/>

#### Thin down the joker pool mid-run!

</div>

A supplemental mod designed to counterbalance the 'bloat' from adding content mods.

- Adds the Executioner, a tarot card that lets you permanently remove jokers in the Shop (or in Packs) for a quick profit!

- Also adds three Jokers of questionable quality! (disable-able in settings)

- Comes with [Fool's Display](https://github.com/dee9c-git/Balatro-FoolsDisplay) support!
